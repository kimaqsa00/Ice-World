Abstract
IceWorld is a modern and responsive ice cream website developed to provide users with an attractive platform for exploring premium ice cream products. The website focuses on delivering an engaging user experience through an elegant interface, responsive layouts, interactive elements, and smooth navigation.
The project has been developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript without relying on external frameworks. The website includes multiple pages such as Home, Menu, Categories, Contact Us, and Order. Each page has been carefully designed to provide useful information while maintaining a professional appearance.
The main objective of the project is to demonstrate front-end development skills, responsive web design principles, and JavaScript functionality by building a real-world business website.

Chapter 1
Introduction
The internet has transformed the way businesses communicate with customers. Nowadays, attractive and responsive websites play an important role in promoting products and services. Ice cream businesses also require professional websites to display their products and allow customers to explore available flavors easily.
The IceWorld website has been developed to represent a premium ice cream brand through a modern, responsive, and visually appealing interface. The project emphasizes usability, attractive layouts, and interactive components that enhance the overall user experience.
The website enables customers to browse various products, view different categories, contact the business, and place orders through an easy-to-use interface.

Chapter 3
Problem Statement
Many small ice cream businesses do not have professional websites to showcase their products online. Customers often face difficulties finding product information, available flavors, pricing, and contact details.
Traditional methods of promotion have limited reach and do not provide an engaging customer experience. Therefore, there is a need for a responsive website that presents products professionally while improving accessibility and customer interaction.
